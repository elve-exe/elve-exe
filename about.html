About me

My name is Elvina and I have a passion.

I grew up in a small town in Lithuania where not many people had an interest in video games. Where they were seen as a silly distraction, a waste of time even. Except for my older sister who grabbed me with her and pulled me into these wast worlds created for joy, for understanding, for connection. The first time i found myself in such a game, i knew i wanted to join the creative hands that make them.

I started exploring these worlds more and more. Oh and the stories i found, the journeys i have been on, the friends i made. It was not just entertainment, and definitely not a waste of time, it was a change that each one of them brought in me. I believe these are some of the most beautiful things games can do. They stay with you. And that is what i want to be a part of. That is why i learnt to create games.
