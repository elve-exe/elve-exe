    • Digital Art

    I started my journey of digital drawing in 2018 when i first held a drawing tablet. I have been playing around with different art-styles and techniques since, building up the skills that now allow me to create and express stories in many different styles of drawing.

    Creating for Others

    On my journey I had opportunities in creating art for other people as well. It brings me purpose and joy being able to create for and work with people, witnessing these projects take life.

    Creating for streamers.

    I learned to enjoy stepping out of my comfort zone when it comes to creating art and make something completely out of my style in order to fit the user needs. This way I've created twitch emotes, posters and profile illustrations for amazing creators.

    Creating for a small serious game developer company Cloudwhale.

    I received some great experience working for Cloudwhale as it was my real job. Turning a simple line of words such as "a dog made out of sticks" into something visual was at first scary but then turned really fun. I got to understand the work process better and see how my skills can help develop something collaborative.

    Game Development

    "Leaves"

    In 2021 i had a chance to create my own game. Through that project i discovered how much i really love making games and how magical it is to finally see it take life.

    This project i called "Leaves". It was inspired by the book "House of Leaves" by Mark Z. Danielewski.

    I was able to create a small demo for this game completely from scratch for the first time and i am still proud of it.

    An Interactive 3D Brain Model

    In 2025 for my Game Design graduation project at the Hanze University of Applied Sciences i collaborated with professors of the University of Groningen to develop an interactive digital learning tool tailored for their students taking the course "Introduction to Clinical Neuropsychology". The tool was designed to support students in learning, understanding, and retaining the complex concepts of the course through interactive and engaging elements.

    This was my biggest project as of yet, as it required extremely thorough and extensive research, iterative exploration and a well planned and executed design plan. The result was a prototype which allowed students to explore and interact with the anatomy of the human brain and test their knowledge through implemented quizzes in relation to their course.

    It was a great project to work on. Here i really hardened my skills in research and development, allowing me to graduate in studies of Game Design with confidence in my abilities and experience which would help continue on beyond university grounds.

    "Greenhouse"

    In 2023 i worked on a serious game meant for people with general anxiety disorder symptoms.

    I called this project "Greenhouse". After thorough research into the disorder as well as different treatments and therapies that are currently applied, i have created a demo for a soothing and relaxing game that would try to help such people.

    The process of working on the project was a mind opening experience for me, since it showed me for the first time how many different ways we can help people through games.

    It strengthened my wish to make an impact through such ways.

    "Symphony of Stars"

    In 2025 i was part of a team of developers collaborating with a concert hall Spot Groningen and a local orchestra to create an immersive, interactive game experience designed for live music events.

    For this project i got to involve myself in every aspect of game design, but major contributions came through research, art and animation.

    It was truly lovely to see people interact and have fun with our game. It was heartwarming to see them leave the room with smiles on their faces.
